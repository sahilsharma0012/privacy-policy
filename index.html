At Intelligence.DigitalAI, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our social media automation and digital marketing services. By using our services, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our services, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Your name, email address, and contact information
  • Facebook and Instagram account information (pages, profile data)
  • Social media access tokens required to manage your accounts
  • Content you provide for publishing (posts, images, captions)
  • Usage data and analytics related to your social media pages
  • Business information such as your company name and industry

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Publish and schedule posts on your Facebook and Instagram accounts on your behalf
  • Manage and automate your social media presence as per your instructions
  • Respond to comments and messages on your social media pages
  • Provide analytics and performance reports for your accounts
  • Communicate with you about our services, updates, and support
  • Improve and optimize our automation workflows and tools

3. Meta Platform Data Usage

Our services integrate with Meta's platforms (Facebook and Instagram) through the official Meta API. We access your Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts only with your explicit permission. Specifically:

  • We only access data necessary to provide our social media management services
  • We do not sell your Meta account data to any third parties
  • Access tokens are stored securely and used only for authorized actions
  • You can revoke our access at any time through your Facebook Settings under Apps and Websites
  • We comply with all Meta Platform Policies and Terms of Service

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Meta/Facebook as required to provide social media management services
  • With automation tools (such as n8n) used internally to manage your accounts
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • With your explicit written consent

5. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes:

  • Encrypted storage of access tokens and sensitive credentials
  • Restricted access to your account data — only authorized team members can access it
  • Regular security reviews of our systems and workflows
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all data transmission

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. When you terminate our services, we will delete your access tokens and personal data within 30 days of your request.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to withdraw consent and revoke app access at any time
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies
